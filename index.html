Critical Holder FAQ

The questions a careful holder should ask before buying, holding, or relying on Holder Rewards.

Are Holder Rewards guaranteed income?
No. Rewards depend on eligible trading fees and the live Pump.fun mechanism. Pump.fun's terms state that rewards have no guaranteed minimum and may be zero. Holding $NGAKAK does not guarantee profit or a return.
What does the $20 eligibility threshold mean?
Current Holder Rewards reporting describes eligibility as holding more than $20 worth of the participating token. The exact threshold and eligibility rules should be checked against the live Pump.fun configuration at launch because platform mechanics can change.
Where do the rewards come from?
For Holder Rewards tokens, the relevant fees are directed to a Pump.fun distribution wallet and distributed pro-rata to eligible holders several times per hour. The amount available depends on actual fee-generating activity.
Does holding longer guarantee a larger payout?
Do not treat longer holding as a guaranteed return. Pump.fun describes Holder Rewards as a holding-based distribution mechanism, but the payout amount still depends on the live rules, eligible balance, and fees generated.
Does NGAKAK GLOBAL control the reward rules?
No. The reward mechanism is a Pump.fun platform mechanism. NGAKAK GLOBAL can publish its intended launch configuration, but platform-level fees, eligibility, and distribution rules must be verified on Pump.fun and can change.
Can the reward system ever change?
Pump.fun's current terms say its services and reward-related mechanisms may be modified, suspended, or discontinued. The site therefore should never promise that a particular reward rate or threshold will remain unchanged forever.
How do I know I am buying the real $NGAKAK?
Use the contract address published in the NGAKAK GLOBAL Trust Center and official channels, then match the address on Pump.fun before swapping. Never use a CA sent by an unsolicited DM, reply, or unofficial account.
Will Holder Rewards protect me if the token price falls?
No. Rewards are not price protection. The market price can rise or fall independently of reward distributions, and rewards can be insufficient to offset a loss in token value.
Is $NGAKAK audited or guaranteed safe?
Do not assume either. Unless NGAKAK GLOBAL publishes a verifiable audit or security review, the site should not claim that the token is audited or safe. A verified contract address reduces impersonation risk but does not eliminate market, smart-contract, platform, or wallet risks.
Will the team ever ask for my seed phrase or private key?
Never. NGAKAK GLOBAL should never ask for a seed phrase, private key, wallet password, or a direct transfer to unlock rewards. Any request like this should be treated as a scam.
Will NGAKAK rug pull?
No website can honestly guarantee future behavior simply by making a promise. NGAKAK GLOBAL should earn trust through verifiable controls: publish the official contract, disclose relevant team/treasury wallets when applicable, show on-chain information, and never ask holders to send funds or credentials. Any claim about liquidity, wallet control, or contract permissions should be independently verifiable.

Yes, the published roadmap describes a progression from Foundation and Fair Launch to Community Growth, The NGAKAK Universe, and Expansion. That includes building The NGAKAK Five, stories, comics, animations, digital content, community experiences, collaborations, and merchandise. The roadmap is a stated plan—not a guarantee that every future milestone will be completed.
