The Honest Holder FAQ

The tough questions, answered straight — no hype, no spin, just what you should know before you buy, hold, or count on Holder Rewards.

Are Holder Rewards guaranteed income?
No — and we won't pretend otherwise. Rewards depend on trading fees and Pump.fun's live mechanism, and can be zero. Holding $NGAKAK never guarantees profit or return.
What does the $20 eligibility threshold mean?
Right now, Holder Rewards eligibility kicks in once your position is worth at least $20. That threshold lives on Pump.fun's side, not ours — always check their live configuration at launch, since platform rules can shift.
Where do the rewards come from?
Trading fees flow into Pump.fun's distribution wallet, then get split pro-rata among eligible holders several times an hour. How much you see depends entirely on real trading activity — we don't control the tap.
Does holding longer guarantee a bigger payout?
Not a guarantee — think better odds, not a fixed return. Pump.fun rewards holding, but your actual payout still depends on live rules, your eligible balance, and the fees generated.
Does NGAKAK GLOBAL control the reward rules?
No, and we think that's a good thing. Holder Rewards runs on Pump.fun's infrastructure — we can share our intended setup, but the real fees, eligibility, and payouts are enforced on their side, checkable independent of anything we say.
Can the reward system ever change?
Yes. Pump.fun's own terms allow them to modify, pause, or discontinue reward mechanics. So we'll never promise you a fixed rate or threshold forever — that's not a promise we could actually keep.
How do I know I am buying the real $NGAKAK?
Match the CA published right here on this page or on our official X account against what you see on Pump.fun before you swap. If it came from a DM, a reply, or an account we don't list as official, treat it as fake.
Will Holder Rewards protect me if the token price falls?
No. Rewards aren't insurance. Price moves independently of reward payouts, and rewards can easily be smaller than a drop in token value.
Is $NGAKAK audited or guaranteed safe?
Don't assume it is. We haven't published a third-party audit, so we won't call the token "safe." A verified CA lowers your risk of buying a fake token — it doesn't remove market, contract, platform, or wallet risk.
Will NGAKAK GLOBAL ever ask for my seed phrase or private key?
Never. Not for verification, not to "unlock" rewards, not for any reason. If someone claiming to be us asks for it, that's a scam — full stop.
Will NGAKAK rug pull?
We can't honestly promise the future — no one can, no matter how sincerely they say it. What we can do is earn your trust: publish the real contract, keep on-chain activity visible, and never ask you for funds or credentials. Verify us — don't just take our word for it.

Yes — our roadmap runs from Foundation and Fair Launch through Community Growth, The NGAKAK Universe, and Expansion, including The NGAKAK Five's stories, comics, and community-built content. It's our stated plan, not a guarantee every milestone lands on schedule.
