Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

NGAKAK GLOBAL respects your privacy. This policy explains the limited information collected through this website and how it is used.

Information we collect

If you choose to use the launch notification form, we collect the email address you provide. We do not ask for wallet seed phrases, private keys, wallet passwords, payment details, or identity documents.

How we use your email

Your email address is used only to send a notification about the NGAKAK launch. We do not sell it or use it for unrelated marketing. You may request that we delete your email by contacting us.

Formspree

The notification form sends your email directly to Formspree, a third-party form-processing service. Formspree processes the submission under its own privacy practices. Please review Formspree's policies if you would like to understand its handling of submitted data.

Hosting and technical data

Like most websites, our hosting and service providers may process limited technical information required to deliver and secure the website, such as request logs. We do not use the launch form to collect more information than the email address you submit.

Your choices

  • You do not have to submit your email to use the website.
  • You may ask about, correct, or request deletion of your submitted email.
  • You may use the official community channels instead of the notification form.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact ngakakglobal@tuta.io.

This policy is provided for transparency about the website's current email-notification flow. It is not legal advice.
